The Context

Post-Corporal Punishment
Botswana Needs New Tools.

With the abolishment of corporal punishment in Botswana schools, a critical gap emerged. Rising classroom disruptions, low parental visibility, and overwhelmed teachers created urgent need for collaborative, non-punitive accountability.

Tshwaragano was built to fill that gap — replacing fear with communication, and silence with shared accountability.

Restorative Discipline

Beyond Punishment —
Towards Accountability

Tshwaragano replaces the cane with communication. Conduct entries are shared with parents instantly, enabling home reinforcement of school values.

Teachers flag students early, counsellors are looped in automatically, and parents are partners in resolution — not just recipients of punishment notices.
